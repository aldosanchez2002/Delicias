Frequently
Asked
Questions

Q- What happens with the leftover food?

A- All remaining food is covered and left with you to keep.

Q- Can I still change my menu after booking?

A- Yes — menu changes are welcome with at least two weeks' notice.

Q- What if my guests arrive after the one-hour service window has ended?

A- Service can be extended for an additional-hour fee of $120.00.

Q- Can you serve a dish that I provide?

A- For quality and food-safety reasons, we serve only dishes prepared by our kitchen.

Q- Can I provide my own plates and utensils? Will the price change?

A- You are welcome to provide your own plates and utensils — pricing remains the same.

Q- Do you offer tastings?

A- Yes — tastings are available and priced as listed on the menu.
