Temporary tool. For each studio photo, pick the kind of plate, then check every item shown on it. Entrees are optional — leave them unchecked for a sides-only or salad-only photo. Labeled photos are shown to customers whose plate matches. Bread rolls and salsa come with every package, so there is no need to label them.

How photos are stored: each photo is one record holding its own image link, so filenames carry no meaning and nothing here has to be numbered or kept in order. Renaming or replacing an image in storage cannot detach its label. The numbers below are just positions in this list.
